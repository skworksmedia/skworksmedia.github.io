(05) — The person behind the work

Behind the lens.

Filming · Editing · Creating

Hey, I'm SK.

A freelance videographer, editor and content creator based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

I started SK.WORKS because I genuinely enjoy filming, editing and turning ideas into something people want to watch.

But I don't want to just show up, shoot, send over a video and call it a day. I'd rather understand what you're trying to achieve, what you're struggling with and how we can make something useful together.

Sometimes it's helping people understand your business better. Sometimes it's showing a side of your brand they haven't seen before. Every project is different, and I like figuring it out with the people I work with.
